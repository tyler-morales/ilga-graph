How we pick targets
Your Senator & Representative
ZIP matched to IL Senate/House districts via Census.
Power Broker
Committee chair for your topic, or highest Moneyball? senator.

Moneyball is a 0–100 composite score combining passage rate, bill progress, co-sponsor pull, cross-party work, network centrality, and institutional role. We use it to rank senators and find high-impact targets.

Score = 0.24×P+ 0.16×L+ 0.16×C+ 0.12×X+ 0.12×N+ 0.20×I
P
Passage rate: laws passed ÷ laws filed (HB/SB only).
L
Pipeline depth: how far the member’s bills go on average (0–6 scale, normalized to 0–1).
C
Co-sponsor pull: avg co-sponsors per law, normalized vs the member with the most.
X
Cross-party: share of bills with at least one opposite-party co-sponsor.
N
Network centrality: share of colleagues the member co-sponsors with.
I
Institutional power: bonus for leadership (President/Leader 1.0, Chair 0.5, Whip 0.25).
Potential Ally
Senator who sits next to yours with the highest cross-party rate.
Super Ally
When the Power Broker and Potential Ally are the same person — both top influence and a seatmate of your senator. Uniquely high-value target.

The chips on each card (e.g. passage rate, bipartisan reach, committee chair) show why we recommend them, not how we picked the category.
