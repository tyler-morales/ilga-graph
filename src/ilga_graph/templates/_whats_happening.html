What's happening and how to fix it

What

Registration denied or revoked. Illinois is applying 625 ILCS 5/3-401(c-1) to treat kei vehicles as non-highway.

Why

Statutory interpretation gap. This is a statutory clarification issue, not a paperwork issue.

Fix

Narrow clarification amendment. A narrow amendment can restore normal registration while keeping safety, equipment, and insurance requirements intact.
