The job

Campaign money is the medium of access. When a hearing is tomorrow or a client asks who funded the sponsors on a bill, you need the sitting-member picture — not a spreadsheet of every receipt in the state.

  • Look up a legislator and see matched candidate-committee receipts.
  • Look up a bill and see who funded the sponsors and voters on it.
  • Decide what to Watch, Ask, or Flag before you walk in.

What this is — and is not

SBE campaign receipts only
Official Illinois State Board of Elections Committees and Receipts, matched to sitting member IDs.
Not earmarked to bills
Bill views show who funded the people on the bill. They do not say who paid for the bill.
Moneyball is a different product
Moneyball on this site is a legislative-effectiveness score. It is not campaign finance.
No lobbyist-registration overlay
This portal does not claim lobbyist filings, expenditures, independent expenditures, 527s, or federal money.
