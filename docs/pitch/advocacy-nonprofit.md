# Turn your coalition into legislator contact that counts

**For:** Coalition leads, EDs, and volunteer coordinators at 501(c)(3)/501(c)(4) organizations and issue coalitions.

---

## The problem

You have a clear ask and a message that works. What you don’t have is a reliable way to turn supporters into *systematic* legislator contact. People don’t know who to call, what to say, or whether it matters. When you talk to legislators or funders, you can’t point to real numbers—calls made, emails sent, offices warmed up. You’re flying blind, and your best asset (your people) is underused.

## Why this works

- **Same message everywhere.** Mission, vision, and your 3–5 conclusions live in one place. The advocacy tool, legislator brief, and Issue page all use them—so every volunteer and every contact stays on message.
- **One ZIP, then go.** Supporters enter a ZIP and get their district senator, representative, and one high-leverage target (the **Power Broker**). No hunting for districts or capitol directories. Call and email in under a minute, with a script and template that match your one-pager.
- **Progress that sticks.** Outreach is tracked (signed-in or in the browser). A simple goal: four district actions (call and email senator and rep), then two Power Broker actions. People see their progress; you see conversion and volume.
- **Campaign updates that move people.** Send Major/Minor updates to subscribers; the public updates page shows where the campaign stands. Keep the coalition aligned and ready when the bill moves.
- **Success you can name.** We measure what you control: constituent contacts, co-sponsors secured, witness slips filed, coalition readiness. No petition theater—real contact, real data. Methodology aligned with *Hardball Lobbying for Nonprofits*.

## What you get

- ZIP lookup → your district senator, representative, and Power Broker (high-influence senator)
- Guided call/email drawer with prewritten script and email template (your message)
- Outreach tracking and goal progress (4 district actions, then 2 Power Broker actions)
- Legislator brief page and one-pager PDFs for staff
- The Issue page: narrative, FAQs, state table, CTA to the advocacy tool
- Campaign email updates and public updates page with timeline
- Admin dashboard: at-a-glance stats, send updates, users, outreach conversion (e.g. drawer-to-outreach, last 90 days)
- Optional: Intelligence (bills, members, coalitions) and Power Map for deeper context

## Proof

We built this for a real Illinois campaign (The Land of Kei). The platform is issue-agnostic; the methodology comes from *Hardball*—strategic message, 3–5 conclusions, constituent-first contact, and success metrics that coalitions can actually deliver. We don’t claim to pass bills for you. We claim to turn your coalition into disciplined constituent contact and give you the numbers to prove it.
