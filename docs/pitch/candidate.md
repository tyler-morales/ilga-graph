# Your issue. One message. Legislator contact that you can show.

**For:** Candidates running for an Illinois seat (and campaign managers) who want a disciplined ground game tied to a specific issue.

---

## The problem

Your campaign has an issue. Volunteers need to contact sitting legislators (or key offices) about it—but without a system, message drift is inevitable and you have no visibility into who’s been contacted or how. You need a ground game that stays on message and produces numbers you can talk about: completion rates, offices reached, and a clear path from supporter to constituent contact.

## Why this works

- **One ZIP, then act.** Supporters enter a ZIP and get their district senator, representative, and one high-leverage target (the **Power Broker**). One script, one email template—your message, everywhere.
- **Progress you can see.** Call and email completion is tracked per legislator. Goal: four district actions (call and email senator and rep), then two Power Broker actions. Volunteers see their checklist; you see completion rates and which offices have been reached.
- **Campaign updates keep people aligned.** Send Major/Minor updates to your list; the public updates page shows where the campaign stands. No mixed messages—one timeline, one story.
- **Numbers when it matters.** Outreach stats (drawer opens, calls, emails, last 90 days) give you something concrete for staff, press, or donors: “We didn’t just post—we got X people to contact the right legislators.”

## What you get

- ZIP lookup → district senator, representative, and Power Broker
- Guided call/email drawer with your script and email template
- Outreach tracking and goal progress (4 district, then 2 Power Broker)
- Legislator brief page and one-pager PDFs for offices and staff
- The Issue page: narrative, FAQs, state table, CTA to the advocacy tool
- Campaign email updates and public updates page with timeline
- Admin dashboard: subscribers, send updates, outreach conversion and volume

## Proof

Built for Illinois. Methodology from *Hardball Lobbying for Nonprofits*—constituent contact and repetition win. This isn’t vague “grassroots”; it’s a system that shows you’re serious and organized. You’re not just talking about the issue; you’re turning supporters into disciplined constituent contacts with the right legislators, and you have the numbers to prove it.
