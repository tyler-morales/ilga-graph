# Give your clients a constituent-contact machine that runs on your message

**For:** Professional lobbyists (firms or in-house) who need clients to show constituent demand and office engagement.

---

## The problem

Clients expect grassroots results. Generic tools don’t target the right members or keep everyone on message. You have no clean way to show who was contacted and how offices responded—so you’re left with “we sent an alert” instead of conversion and warm offices. You need a system that runs on *your* strategy and delivers data you can report.

## Why this works

- **Your message, our engine.** You (or the coalition) own the one-pager and scripts. The platform handles lookup (district + **Power Broker**), guided call/email, and tracking. You stay on strategy; the tool runs operations.
- **Right targets.** Power Broker is a high-leverage senator surfaced by the same analytics we use for the Intelligence dashboard—not guesswork. Constituents reach their district members first, then the one member who can move the needle.
- **Conversion and funnel metrics.** Drawer opens, calls completed, emails sent—and optional “how interested did they seem?” so you see which offices are warming up. Report to clients with numbers, not anecdotes.
- **Community-sourced contact info.** When callers get an office email that isn’t on ILGA, they can submit it. The next advocate sees it pre-filled. You get better coverage without extra legwork.

## What you get

- Same capability set as coalition-facing deployments: ZIP lookup, district + Power Broker, guided call/email drawer, outreach tracking
- Legislator brief page and PDFs for staff—one link to send, one story to tell
- Admin dashboard: users, subscribers, outreach stats (conversion rates and volumes, last 90 days)
- Optional Intelligence: bills to watch, power movers, coalition landscape, member/bill deep-dives—committee and bill context when you need it
- GraphQL API for members, bills, committees, votes, witness slips, and search if you want to integrate or analyze

## Proof

Built on real Illinois General Assembly data and ML-style analytics (Moneyball, influence). Designed for how lobbying actually works: right targets, repetition of the same message, and evidence of constituent contact. We don’t overpromise outcomes—we deliver a constituent-contact machine and the data that proves it ran.
