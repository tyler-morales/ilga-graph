# App overview

The ILGA Graph app exposes Illinois General Assembly data and ML-backed analytics through a web UI and a GraphQL API. The deployed site is branded as **The Land of Kei** (advocacy group / nonprofit); the name appears in the browser title, footer, share cards, and verification emails.

Submitting the form redirects to `/advocacy/?zip=...`. Below the hero, **What's happening** is a single row of three parts (What / Why / Fix) in plain English. **Strategic plan (Hardball Ch 5):** **Mission** (one sentence with truck watermark); **Vision** (one full-width Vision statement); **Our message in 5 points** is a card grid with one icon and one point per card. | **Advocacy** | `/advocacy` | Landing is built for high conversion: same hero as home (threat-focused copy, one-field ZIP form, ticker; trust badges removed). Hero image is configurable via `static/images/truck-hero.jpg` (fallback if missing). ZIP-based lookup: find your legislators and recommended targets. Results show **Your district members** (Senator and Representative side-by-side on one row), then **Strategic Targets** (Power Broker) below. The sidebar includes **Your goal**: a clear 4-action checklist (call and email your Senator, call and email your Representative) with a **gamified progress bar** (“X of 4 actions complete”). Each action has a **Call** or **Email** link that opens the advocacy drawer to the right step. When **all 4 district actions are complete** (call and email both Senator and Representative), the goal section advances to a second phase: the heading line becomes **"Your goal: Contact the Power Broker"** (or **"This week's goal"** when an active campaign is present), a **Completed goals** block shows the four district steps struck out and at lower opacity, a short **Who is the Power Broker?** explainer appears, and a new progress bar and checklist show **2 actions** (call and email the Power Broker). The celebration/CTA block ("You've reached both your district members" / "Want more impact? Call the Power Broker") has been removed; the Power Broker is now the automatic next goal. Each card has a single **Reach out** button that opens the advocacy drawer. The drawer has a **Call** and **Email** step strip (text links) so you can switch between the call script and the email template at any time; the recommended path is **call first, then email** (follow-up). Opening "Reach out" defaults to the Call step, or to Email if you've already called that legislator. Call script and email drawer use the same prefilled templates; the email view supports **Follow-up** (after a call) or **First outreach**. **ZIP and constituent status** are shared—edit in either step and the other updates when you switch. In the email step, changing the **City or ZIP** mad lib to a different 5-digit ZIP updates the **I'm a constituent** checkbox automatically. |

## Data flow (high level)

1. **Scrape** — Scripts hit ilga.gov; results cached in `cache/` (members, bills, committees, votes, etc.).
2. **Load** — On startup the app loads cache (and optionally runs ML pipeline); in dev, can fall back to `mocks/dev/`.
3. **Serve** — FastAPI serves HTML (Jinja2) and GraphQL (Strawberry). No scraping on request.

- **Feature flags:** Client-facing toggles (e.g. `ILGA_FEATURE_MESSAGE_MARQUEE`, `ILGA_FEATURE_IMAGES_MARQUEE`) are in `config.py`; prod defaults are conservative; override with `ILGA_FEATURE_<NAME>=1` in `.env` if needed.
- **Readiness:** Use `GET /health`; response is `ready` when members are loaded.

## Key concepts

- **Strategic plan** — Mission, vision, and 5-point message live in `content.py` (STRATEGIC_MISSION, STRATEGIC_VISION, STRATEGIC_FIVE_POINTS) and are passed to the home and The Issue pages so both stay on-message (Hardball Ch 5). On the home page, Vision is a single full-width statement; the 5 points use a card grid with Streamline icons (Iconify) and a primary-school color palette (one solid color per card). **Advocacy success goals** — "How we measure success" (constituent contacts, co-sponsors secured, witness slips filed, coalition ready to act) is in the Issue page FAQ “Advocacy & how we work” (FAQ_ADVOCACY, item "success-measures" with STRATEGIC_SUCCESS_MEASURE and STRATEGIC_SUCCESS_MEASURE_ITEMS). Road-legal outcome is the campaign objective; success metrics are under the coalition’s control.
- **Canonical content (no hallucination)** — All substantive copy, key points, and messaging must come only from approved sources: `content.py` (STRATEGIC_*, FAQ_*, BRIEF_*), `legislator_brief.html` (official legislator one-pager text), `the_issue.html` (constituent narrative). The legislator one-pager PDF and constituent brief PDF are the same as these; do not invent content. See `docs/canonical/README.md` and rule `no-hallucination-content`.
- **Moneyball score** — 0–100 composite ranking legislators (passage, pipeline, co-sponsor pull, cross-party, network, institutional). Used for Power Broker and leaderboards; see `metricsGlossary` in GraphQL.
- **Advocacy targets** — For a given ZIP: your senator, your rep, and a “Power Broker” (high influence senator). Optional policy filter by committee.
- **Advocacy copy** — In-app text in the call script, email drawer, no-answer drawer, and result hints is written in a conversational, human tone (e.g. "No public email — we'll guide you to get it on the call") so advocates feel guided, not instructed.
- **Loading facts** — When the ZIP search loading animation is enabled (feature flag), the "finding targets" state shows one random kei truck fact per search (e.g. "Kei means light in Japanese", "Suzuki Carry, Honda Acty, Daihatsu Hijet — classic kei trucks", 660cc engine cap, 25-year import rule) for light gameification while results load.
- **Campaigns (contact periods)** — Admins can create and manage **campaigns** (title, message, ask, target type: all or by district, optional date window). Optional **session milestone** ties a campaign to a deadline from `reference/session_schedule.json` (e.g. HB committee deadline): admin selects "End at session milestone" in the campaign form; `end_at` is set to end of that day and the banner/priority callout can show "Contact before [milestone label] ([date])". At most one campaign is **active** at a time; activating one deactivates others. When an active campaign is visible to a user (all users, or only users in target districts by ZIP), the site shows a **current-action** slim bar (advocacy and other pages) and on **Updates** a **priority callout** with status and next step. The advocacy goal heading becomes **"This week's goal"**. Outreach recorded while a campaign is active is attributed to that campaign (`OutreachEvent.campaign_id`); the client passes the active campaign id from `__ILGA_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGN`. Admin campaign detail and dashboard show outreach counts per campaign. Aligns with Hardball: "Call on them at crucial moments"; targeted segments (by_district). See `campaign_helpers.py`, `session_schedule.get_deadlines_for_campaigns()`, `/admin/campaigns`, and [Site banners](../reference/performance-resilience.md#site-banners-robust-component) for banner/callout behavior.
- **Email update types** — Campaign updates are categorized as **Major** (bill movement, key milestones, urgent CTAs), **Minor** (routine progress, thank-yous, reminders), or **Other** (general news). Admins select the type via radio buttons when composing at `/admin/updates`. The type is displayed on the public updates page: in the sidebar (e.g. "Major · Feb 26, 2026") and above the article title ("Major update", "Minor update", or "Update"). Stored in `Update.update_type`; default for new drafts is "other".

- **Share cards and analytics** — The base template sets Open Graph and Twitter Card meta so shared links show a title, description, and image. Canonical URL and default meta description come from `ILGA_APP_BASE_URL` and config; the site name (default **The Land of Kei**) is used for `og:site_name`, page title, and footer. The default description is cause-tailored for Kei vehicle registration advocacy (625 ILCS 5/3-401(c-1)). Optional [Umami](https://umami.is) analytics: set `ILGA_UMAMI_WEBSITE_ID` (e.g. in prod) to inject the tracking script; no cookies, 100k events/month on the free tier. For search engine discovery, the app serves `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` (both use `ILGA_APP_BASE_URL`).
- **Legal and trust** — The footer links to a **Privacy policy** (what we collect: email — sign-in and campaign-update subscription — outreach you save including campaign attribution when active, account ZIP code, community-sourced legislator emails, bug reports, Umami analytics, usage/conversion measurement, local storage; how we use it; data sharing and no sale; retention; security; cookies; your rights to access/correct/delete; contact) and **Terms of use** (what this site does, disclaimer, not the ILGA, acceptable use, contact). These app pages are served at `/privacy` and `/terms`. The footer also shows a **Last updated** date on all pages. When `ILGA_FOOTER_LAST_UPDATED` and `ILGA_FOOTER_LAST_UPDATED_ISO` are not set, the app derives the date from the last git commit (accurate in dev and after deploy, since deploy runs `git pull`). Set both env vars to override (e.g. for a specific deploy timestamp).
- **User ZIP (single source of truth)** — There is one ZIP: the value the user enters in the hero form. That ZIP drives both the legislator search and **Your goal** (your Senator/Rep). Submitting the hero form updates the URL and refreshes results so the goal and cards stay in sync. When signed in, the app can prefill the hero from `User.zip_code`; updating and submitting a new ZIP can PATCH `/advocacy/api/me/zip` so the next visit prefills from the account.
- **Your goal and progress (signed-in vs signed-out)** — The goal sidebar shows a dynamic goal line: **"Your goal: Contact your district legislators"** (4 actions) or, once those four are complete and a Power Broker exists, **"Your goal: Contact the Power Broker"** (2 actions). When an active campaign is present, the heading shows **"This week's goal"** to align with the campaign banner. Completed district steps appear under "Completed goals" (struck out, lower opacity) with a short "Who is the Power Broker?" explainer. **Signed-in:** Progress is stored on the server (OutreachEvent). The goal sidebar and the member cards both come from the same server context, so they always match. **Signed-out:** Progress is stored in **localStorage** keyed by ZIP. On load, the client applies that data to the goal block and to the member cards. When the client detects 4/4 district actions complete and a Power Broker id is present, it switches the goal section to the Power Broker phase (same two-phase DOM and class toggle as server-rendered). **On sign-in or account creation:** The client runs `mergeLocalStorageGoalToServer()`: for the current ZIP, each call/email in localStorage is POSTed to `/outreach/record` so those actions are saved to the account; then that ZIP’s key is removed from localStorage. After that, the results view is refreshed so the goal and cards reflect server data. New actions after sign-in are always recorded to the account.
- **Outreach data** — When signed in, the app records each call, email, or no-answer in a local SQLite DB (`data/ilga.db`). Each event stores: **time/date** (`created_at`), **legislator** (`member_id`), **kind** (call / email / no_answer), **ZIP**, optional **outcome**, **notes**, **contact name** (person who picked up), **support score** (1–5: opposed → champion), and **constituent** (whether the advocate was a constituent). The call drawer asks "How interested did they seem?" in a **Quick poll** bubble: tap Opposed → Champion (red to gold pills). **The call is recorded when you select an interest level** (poll submit); you then see how many other advocates reported for that office (or "You're the first to report!" if none yet). The default next step is **Draft your follow-up email**; optional **I'll do it later** leaves the call recorded and transitions the drawer to a **done** state: "You're done with [Name]." with a primary CTA to **call your other district member** (Representative or Senator). After you click **I sent it!** for the email, the same done state appears. In both cases, if you only called (no email yet), a soft line reminds you you can come back to send the follow-up email later. The **Your goal** sidebar shows a **Next: Call your [Representative/Senator]** line when exactly one district member has been called, so you're nudged from member 1 → member 2. On the advocacy cards, a single **Reach out** button opens the drawer; when you've both called and emailed a legislator, it shows **Reached out** (muted) so you can see your progress at a glance. **Email outreach** is recorded only when you click **I sent** in the email drawer (after using **Open in email client** and actually sending); verifying your From address does not record outreach. The hero **Sign in** and the email drawer **From** field use the same magic-link flow and UI copy (Send code → 6-digit code → Confirm; same loading states and error messages) for consistency. If you are not signed in, clicking **I sent** does not save; the button shows **Sign in to save this outreach** and the drawer scrolls to the From/verify step so you can sign in and have your next action recorded. See `GET /outreach/my-history` and `POST /outreach/record`. Run `make seed-outreach` to prefill from a seed table.
- **Community-sourced legislator emails** — Many legislators have no public email on ILGA. When a caller records a call and enters the office email in the wrap-up form, that email is stored (for members with no public email) in `community_member_emails`. The next constituent opening the drawer for that legislator sees the email pre-filled and a short line: "This email was shared by another constituent who called this office" (or "Verified by multiple constituents who called" when 2+ callers submitted the same address). No submitter identity is shown. Consensus (same email from multiple callers) is the only verification signal in v1. See `community_email.py`, `POST /outreach/record` (optional `legislator_email`), and the advocacy drawer/wrap-up context.
- **Obsidian vault** — Optional export: `ILGA_Graph_Vault/` with markdown notes and Bases views (generated by exporter, not by the web app by default).
- **Terminology** — Domain terms (campaign, milestone, phase, goal, etc.) are defined in a single source of truth: `DOMAIN_GLOSSARY` in `content.py`. The public **[/glossary](/glossary)** page shows domain terms, session calendar terms, and kei vehicle terms. See [Domain glossary](../reference/glossary.md) for definitions and disambiguation. Use the glossary when writing copy or code to avoid overloaded meanings.
- **Kei poll completion** — The poll form (home "Why you care", /updates?prompt=kei) includes a visible step indicator (step 1 of 2, step 2 of 2), a benefit line ("Your answer helps us understand our community"), and submit button "Submit and see results". The submit button is disabled until the user has answered both questions (branch + option).
- **Sidebar Kei poll** — The same Kei poll (form or results) appears in the **sidebar** on The Issue, Legislator brief, Fact sheet, and Glossary pages: at the bottom of sidebar content, **above** the Share section. It uses a single poll id (`sidebar-kei-poll`) so state (voted vs not, form vs results) is consistent across these pages; context is provided by `get_kei_poll_sidebar_context()` from the updates router.
